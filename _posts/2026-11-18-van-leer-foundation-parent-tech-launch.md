The Parent Launch: OG Assistant borrows the language and conventions of smart-assistant marketing to give everyday parenting skills the launch treatment they deserve.

In a world full of AI Assistants and promises of the next big tech breakthroughs, when it comes to the world’s toughest users - Kids, even the smartest technology can struggle. A Kids request rarely arrives as a perfectly worded prompt. Understanding it takes familiarity, patience and a connection built through countless everyday interactions. But for some reason...
