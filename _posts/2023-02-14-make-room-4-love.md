Make room for Love by<br>donating clothes to the Salvation Army’s ReShare Stores.

Couples often fight about wardrobe space, especially in small Dutch apartments. So on Valentine’s Day, with The Dutch Salvation Army X Reshare Store, we encouraged people to give the most precious gift to their loved ones: some wardrobe space.<br>We created beautiful video, capturing the essence of campaign. But we went even further, we created special gift cards that transform into closet divider and distributed to all over the Netherlands.
