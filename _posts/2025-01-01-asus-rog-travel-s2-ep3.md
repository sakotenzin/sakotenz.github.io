Next stop: Allywood.

The best games take you somewhere else. For the arrival of the ROG Xbox Ally, we turned that promise into a travel pitch - with two familiar faces behind the counter.

This chapter of ROG Travel reunited Grand Theft Auto V actors Ned Luke and Shawn Fonteno inside the fictional agency. Ned returned as the supervisor, while Shawn arrived as a new recruit with the perfect destination in mind: Allywood, a getaway built around the possibilities of gaming.

The ROG Xbox Ally became the ticket.

By treating a gaming handheld as a passport to adventure, the film gave the product’s portability a playful, human story. Travel-agency humour and the duo’s shared gaming history connected the launch to something players already understood: the excitement of stepping into another world.

Part of the continuing ROG Travel series, the campaign brought a new destination to its expanding universe - and a new way for players to take their favourite escapes with them.

Big adventures. Hand luggage.
