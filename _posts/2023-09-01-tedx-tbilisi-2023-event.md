## CONCEPT FOR TEDX TBILISI 2023<br>BREKING POINT
{: .statement}

TED x Tbilisi annual event celebrated 10th anniversary of spreading Idas and connecting minds. For this special occasion, our visual concept was based on line art of map of Tbilisi. Capital of Georgia.<br>For more than two decades, Map has  been a pain of Georgian nation an ongoing war for Georgian land that is occupied by Russia.<br>But maps also has a mean to guide and connect, something that TED x is passionate about. So we decided to take pain and flip it around to celebrate our resilience and pride in our country.
